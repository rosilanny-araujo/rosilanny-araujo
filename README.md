# Rosilanny Carvalho Araujo

**Decision-focused data science:** I build statistical models that help people make better decisions with real data.

I am a master's student in applied statistics at the University of São Paulo (ICMC-USP), with five years of industry analytics experience in retail and education companies. I am applying to PhD programs in statistics, quantitative methods, and decision sciences for Fall 2027.

## Research
- **Efficiency of public education spending** (master's dissertation, ICMC-USP, expected June 2027). Hierarchical Bayesian stochastic frontiers for learning outcomes (Ideb) and spending per student across 606 São Paulo municipalities. Instead of ranking municipalities, I classify them by the posterior probability of being among the most or least efficient, which identified 11 reference and 9 priority municipalities. Advisor: Prof. Cibele Maria Russo. Code will be released after the defense.
- **School prioritization in Minas Gerais.** Single-level and multilevel models for 2,040 state schools, with a calibrated four-level priority classification. Presented at the XXIX National Meeting on Computational Modeling (ENMC), 2026.
- **SAVITS** (IBICT/MCTI). Open Science impact indicators for a federal platform that evaluates social technologies.
